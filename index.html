আমাদের কথা · About

JEXCA সম্পর্কে

ঝিনাইদহ এক্স-ক্যাডেটস অ্যাসোসিয়েশন (জেক্সকা) ঝিনাইদহ ক্যাডেট কলেজের প্রাক্তন ক্যাডেটদের সংগঠন। কলেজ থেকে পাস করা প্রত্যেক ক্যাডেট স্বয়ংক্রিয়ভাবে এর সদস্য, আর বিশ্বজুড়ে এর সদস্য সংখ্যা ২,৫০০ জনের বেশি। সংগঠনটি সমাজসেবা অধিদপ্তরে নিবন্ধিত। দুর্যোগে ত্রাণ, দরিদ্র শিক্ষার্থীদের বৃত্তি ও ঢাকা-খুলনায় ফ্রি ফ্রাইডে ক্লিনিক চালানোর মতো জনকল্যাণমূলক কাজ করে। খুলনার জেক্সকা হেলথকেয়ার কমপ্লেক্সে ৫০,০০০-এর বেশি রোগী বিনামূল্যে চিকিৎসা পেয়েছেন। ঝিনাইদহ ক্যাডেট কলেজ ১৯৬৩ সালের ১৮ অক্টোবর প্রতিষ্ঠিত হয়।

Jhenidah Ex-Cadets Association (JEXCA) is the alumni association of the ex-cadets (former students) of Jhenidah Cadet College (JCC). Every cadet who has passed out of Jhenidah Cadet College is by default a member, and the association counts more than 2,500 members around the world. JEXCA is registered with the Department of Social Services (Social Welfare Department) of Bangladesh.

Beyond keeping its members connected, JEXCA works as a charitable and social body. Its work includes relief after disasters, scholarships for needy students, welfare support for needy JCC employees, and free healthcare for poor people. Its Free Friday Clinic (FFC) gives free consultation and medicine to slum dwellers and poor people in Dhaka and Khulna, and many members donate monthly to keep the clinics running. The JEXCA Healthcare Complex in Khulna had served more than 50,000 underprivileged patients free of cost by 2022. Corporate partners such as IPDC Finance and the MTB Foundation have supported it.

JEXCA also organises the college's reunions. In January 2013 it hosted the three-day Golden Jubilee of Jhenidah Cadet College and the 11th JCC Reunion at the college grounds. Thousands of alumni attended, and Finance Minister Abul Mal Abdul Muhith inaugurated the event as chief guest.

About the parent institution: Jhenidah Cadet College was established on 18 October 1963 and took its first intake in 1964. It is the second oldest of Bangladesh's twelve cadet colleges, which are fully residential public schools with a military-style routine. Its campus of about 103 acres lies on the outskirts of Jhenaidah town in Khulna Division.

লক্ষ্য ও উদ্দেশ্য · Aims

আমাদের লক্ষ্য

  • Keep a lasting bond and an up-to-date record of all ex-cadets of Jhenidah Cadet College
  • Arrange urgent blood donations by linking members who need blood with donors
  • Help unemployed ex-cadets with career support and job connections
  • Provide free healthcare and medicine to underprivileged people through the Free Friday Clinics and the JEXCA Healthcare Complex
  • Support needy students with scholarships and needy JCC employees with welfare
  • Respond to natural disasters with relief work

কার্যক্রম · Activities

আমরা যা করি

◆

Free Friday Clinic (FFC)

Free consultation and medicine for slum dwellers and poor people in Dhaka and Khulna, run by volunteer ex-cadet doctors and paid for by members' monthly donations.

✦

JEXCA Healthcare Complex, Khulna

A voluntary healthcare centre in Khulna that had given free medical consultancy and medication to more than 50,000 underprivileged patients by 2022, with support from partners such as IPDC Finance and the MTB Foundation.

●

JCC Reunions

Large reunions of JCC alumni, including the three-day Golden Jubilee and 11th Reunion held at the college grounds in January 2013.

▲

Scholarships and Welfare

Scholarships for needy students and welfare support for needy employees of Jhenidah Cadet College.

■

Disaster Relief

Relief work for people affected by natural disasters.

★

Blood Donation and Member Network

The JEXCA mobile app lets members send instant blood requests by SMS, contact donors, follow events and notices, and find career help.
